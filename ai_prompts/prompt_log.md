# AI & Prompt Engineering Log - Smart Attendance & Performance Analyzer

## Overview
This log documents the prompts and AI decision logic integrated into **Project 17: Smart Attendance & Performance Analyzer** for first-year JNTUK R23 B.Tech AI & DS students.

---

## 1. AI Correlation & Trend Projection Prompting

### System Persona
> You are an AI Academic Advisor for JNTUK R23 B.Tech Artificial Intelligence and Data Science students. Your task is to correlate student attendance rates with academic performance and generate personalized, actionable mentoring recommendations.

### Statistical Modeling Logic
- **Input Features**: `Attendance_Pct`, `Mid1_Marks`, `Mid2_Marks`, `Assignment_Marks`, `EndSem_Marks`
- **Pearson Correlation ($r$)**: Measures the linear dependency between attendance percentage and final exam score.
- **OLS Trend Line Equation**: $y = m \cdot x + c$ where $m$ is the performance increment per attendance percentage point.

## 2. Rule-Based AI Performance Classification Prompts

| Category Tag | Threshold Condition | Target Mentoring Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Consistent High-Achiever 🌟** | Attendance $\ge 85\%$ AND Composite Score $\ge 80\%$ | Peer mentoring, research project assignment |
| **High Potential, Needs Attendance Boost ⚠️** | Attendance $< 75\%$ AND Composite Score $\ge 75\%$ | Attendance policy intervention, high capability maintenance |
| **Stable & Regular Performer 👍** | Attendance $\ge 75\%$ AND Composite Score $\ge 60\%$ | Standard academic progression monitoring |
| **At-Risk 🚨** | Attendance $< 75\%$ AND Composite Score $< 60\%$ | Mandatory remedial classes & parent-teacher meet |
| **Needs Performance Improvement 📉** | Attendance $\ge 75\%$ AND Composite Score $< 60\%$ | Academic tutoring in low-scoring subjects |
