🧮 Linear Algebra Feature Dot Product Formulations

Under the JNTUK R23 engineering evaluation criteria, student feature vectors are mathematically transformed using dot product projections.

Feature Vector (S_i) = [ Attendance_%, Mid1, Mid2, External, Backlogs ]
Weight Vector (W) = [ 0.25, 0.15, 0.15, 0.35, -0.10 ]
Composite Score = S_i · W = (Att_val × 0.25) + (Mid1 × 0.15) + (Mid2 × 0.15) + (Ext_val × 0.35) - (Backlogs × 0.10)
Credit-Weighted Score = (Internal Marks × 0.4) + (Attendance % × 0.6)
